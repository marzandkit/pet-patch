# Pet Patch 🐼

A cozy game where you take care of animal friends in your own home.

**Play it:** https://marzandkit.github.io/pet-patch/ (works best on an iPad; tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen" to play it like an app)

## What you can do

- Make your own person and pick animal friends (panda, puppy, kitty, bunny) and give each one a name.
- Take care of them: food, water, play, a bath and a nap. Nothing bad ever happens, so take your time.
- Walk around a house with a bathroom, bedroom, living room and kitchen. Your pets follow you.
- Grow bamboo, carrots, berries and flowers in the garden, or get free food at the Honey Bear store.
- Cook on the stove (an apple becomes apple pie!) and let hot food cool on the table.
- Take your pet on a walk, throw a ball to play fetch, or go fishing in the river.
- Earn coins by caring for your pets, then buy furniture, treats and dress-up clothes.
- Earn enough coins to unlock special pets: red panda, fox, koala, penguin and unicorn.
- Day, sunset and night each last 5 minutes. Sleep in your bed to make the night go by fast.
- Make up to 6 worlds, and invite a friend from one world to visit another.

## How to play

- **Tap** the ground to walk there.
- **Tap** furniture to use it (sit, sleep, open the fridge, turn on the lamp).
- **Drag** your person onto the bathtub, bed or sofa to use it.
- **Drag** furniture to move it. Drag it to the edge of the screen to move it to the next room.
- Use the buttons at the bottom to go to other rooms, the garden or the store.
- Tap the pause button to stop time or go back to your worlds.

Your worlds are saved in the browser on your device.
